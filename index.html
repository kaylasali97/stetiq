About

The STETIQ Approach

Esthetics, Elevated.

STETIQPronounced: steh-teek
Esthetics, Elevated.

STETIQ is a contemporary esthetics brand founded on the belief that skincare should be intentional, individualised and approached with expertise. Created to offer a more considered experience of professional skin treatments, STETIQ brings together technical knowledge, carefully selected skincare and purposeful treatment techniques.

The intention is to move beyond a conventional facial experience. Every treatment is approached with attention to the individual's skin profile, concerns and desired outcomes, placing consultation, personalisation and professional care at the centre of the experience.

STETIQ represents a refined approach to esthetics: where knowledge meets technique, and skincare becomes an experience in its own right.

The Founder

Kayla S. Ali

STETIQ was founded by Kayla, whose experience spans over a decade in the beauty industry. With professional training and certification in cosmetology and esthetics through Pivot Point International, alongside P.Louise Pro certification in makeup artistry and many others, she brings together technical knowledge, hands-on experience and a strong appreciation for the artistry of beauty.

Her background across multiple areas of the beauty industry has shaped an approach that values both the science and the aesthetic of professional treatments. She believes that exceptional skincare begins with understanding the individual, recognising that no two complexions or treatment journeys are exactly alike.

Through STETIQ, she has created a space dedicated to considered skincare, purposeful techniques and personalised esthetic experiences. Her intention is simple: to elevate the standard of the treatment experience through knowledge, attention to detail and an appreciation for the individuality of every client.

Services

The STETIQ Service Menu

Every treatment at STETIQ is approached with consideration for the individual's skin, concerns and desired results. Explore the treatment collections below to discover an experience suited to your skincare needs.

I

The Facial Collection

  • The Purity Edit

    A complexion-refining treatment designed to address excess oil, congestion and visible impurities. This deep-cleansing experience helps purify the skin, refine its appearance and restore a fresher, more balanced-looking complexion.

  • The Hydra Edit

    A moisture-intensive treatment dedicated to replenishing and restoring the skin's hydration. Designed to comfort dry, depleted complexions, this nourishing experience helps improve the appearance of skin smoothness, suppleness and luminosity while supporting a healthy-looking moisture barrier.

  • The Rejuvenation Edit

    A restorative firming treatment designed to address visible signs of ageing and promote a smoother, more refined complexion. Featuring specialised exfoliation, targeted massage and advanced skin-conditioning preparations, this treatment focuses on improving the appearance of firmness, elasticity and fine lines.

  • The Luminescence Edit

    An illuminating complexion treatment designed to improve the appearance of uneven skin tone, discolouration and post-blemish marks. A targeted brightening experience that encourages a more refined, radiant and visibly even-looking complexion.

  • The Quattro Edit

    An elevated facial featuring a multi-layered approach to skin nourishment and rejuvenation. This immersive treatment combines seaweed-based preparations, specialised massage techniques and a mineral-rich thermal mask to leave the complexion feeling replenished, refreshed and revitalised.

IIThe STETIQ Signature

The STETIQ Signature Edit

An exclusive, fully customised facial experience curated around the individual's skin profile, concerns and treatment goals. Combining carefully selected professional skincare, targeted techniques and specialised enhancements, this bespoke treatment delivers a considered approach to skin nourishment, refinement and rejuvenation.

The experience includes a comprehensive skin consultation and assessment, individually selected professional skincare, customised treatment techniques, a targeted mask and finishing preparations, and personalised post-treatment recommendations.

III

The Custom Treatment Collection

  • The Silhouette Edit

    A precision dermaplaning exfoliation treatment designed to remove accumulated dead skin cells and fine vellus hair from the skin's surface. This treatment reveals a smoother, softer-looking complexion while enhancing surface radiance and creating a beautifully refined skin texture.

  • The Contour Edit

    A specialised facial massage experience that encourages lymphatic flow and promotes facial relaxation. Through intentional massage techniques, this treatment helps reduce the appearance of puffiness while leaving the complexion looking refreshed and the facial contours appearing more defined.

  • The Collagen Edit

    A professional skin-rejuvenation treatment utilising controlled microneedling to create precise microchannels within the skin, stimulating its natural wound-healing response. This treatment is designed to support improvements in the appearance of skin texture, fine lines and certain types of superficial acne scarring over a course of appropriately spaced sessions.

  • The Cryo Edit

    A distinctive cooling-focused facial experience incorporating specialised cold-therapy tools, ice globes, cool steam and the BaByliss cryotherapy brush. This refreshing treatment uses controlled cooling sensations to soothe the skin, temporarily minimise the appearance of puffiness and leave the complexion feeling revitalised.

  • The Resurfacing Edit

    A mechanical exfoliation treatment designed to gently abrade the outermost layer of accumulated dead skin cells, encouraging a smoother and more polished-looking complexion. This surface-refining experience helps improve the appearance of dullness, uneven texture and superficial congestion while promoting a fresher-looking skin finish.
